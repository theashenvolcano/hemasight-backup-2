About the Project

About Us

A student-led medical AI research project exploring camera-based technology for preliminary, noninvasive anemia screening.

Our Methodology

Our Research Approach

We are exploring how imaging and relevant medical data could be studied together to investigate noninvasive anemia screening.

👁️

Camera-Based Eye Imaging

We study images of the palpebral conjunctiva, the tissue lining the inside of the eyelid. Visible characteristics may be investigated for potential associations with hemoglobin levels.

💡

Image Quality and Lighting

Lighting and image quality can affect the colors captured by a camera. Considering these factors can help us investigate the reliability of image-based analysis.

🔬

Hemoglobin Reference Data

When available, laboratory hemoglobin measurements can provide reference values for evaluating whether image-based findings correspond with measured hemoglobin levels.

📋

Relevant Medical Context

Relevant medical biomarkers and optional questionnaire information may provide additional context for research, subject to appropriate privacy safeguards and data availability.

What Guides Us

Our Principles

The values that guide our approach to medical AI research.

🛡️

Scientific Validation

The system must be evaluated against appropriate reference data before its performance or usefulness can be established. We aim to present findings accurately and avoid unsupported medical claims.

🔒

Privacy and Responsibility

Medical information and images should be handled responsibly. Research data should be used only with appropriate permissions and safeguards to protect the privacy of the people represented.

🌎

Healthcare Accessibility

We are interested in whether accessible technology could eventually support earlier screening and further research, particularly where access to traditional testing may be limited.

Medical disclaimer: This project is for research and educational purposes only. It is not a diagnostic tool and must not be used to diagnose, rule out, or treat anemia. Any potential screening method requires appropriate scientific validation and clinical evaluation. Anyone concerned about anemia should consult a qualified healthcare professional.
