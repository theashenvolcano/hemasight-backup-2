Understanding

What Is Anemia?

Anemia is a common medical condition in which the body does not have enough healthy red blood cells or sufficient hemoglobin to carry oxygen efficiently throughout the body. Hemoglobin is the protein found in red blood cells that transports oxygen from the lungs to the body's tissues and organs, allowing them to function properly. When oxygen delivery is reduced, individuals may experience symptoms such as fatigue, weakness, dizziness, pale skin, headaches, shortness of breath, and difficulty concentrating.

Anemia can be caused by a variety of factors, including iron deficiency, vitamin deficiencies, chronic diseases, blood loss, or inherited genetic disorders. Because its symptoms often develop gradually and can easily be mistaken for everyday tiredness or stress, many people remain unaware that they have anemia until the condition becomes more advanced. Early detection is essential, as timely diagnosis and treatment can help prevent serious health complications and improve overall quality of life.

Purpose

Our Mission

Our mission is to make early anemia detection more accessible, affordable, and effective through innovative technology and scientific research. We are developing a solution that explores new ways to identify individuals who may be at risk for anemia before serious symptoms develop. By combining advances in artificial intelligence with medical research, we aim to increase awareness of this common yet often overlooked condition while providing a simple, non-invasive approach to early screening. Our goal is to empower individuals to recognize potential signs of anemia sooner, encourage timely medical evaluation, and ultimately improve health outcomes through earlier detection and intervention.

Impact

Why This Matters

Early detection of anemia is essential because it allows individuals to seek medical evaluation and treatment before the condition becomes more severe. When left undiagnosed or untreated, anemia can negatively affect physical health, cognitive function, academic and workplace performance, and overall quality of life. Although anemia affects millions of people worldwide, many cases go undetected because the symptoms often develop gradually and are easily mistaken for everyday fatigue or stress. Through our research, we aim to explore innovative methods for supporting earlier screening, increasing public awareness, and encouraging timely medical evaluation. By promoting early recognition, we hope to contribute to better health outcomes and help reduce the long-term impact of this common yet frequently overlooked condition.

Our Approach

Key Features

Three pillars guiding our exploration of accessible anemia screening.

👁️

Noninvasive Technology

Our research explores the use of camera-based imaging to investigate whether visible signs associated with anemia can be identified without the need for a blood draw. By analyzing subtle visual features with advanced imaging technology and artificial intelligence, we aim to evaluate the potential of a fast, non-invasive, and accessible approach to support early anemia screening.

🌍

Accessible Screening

Our research investigates whether everyday devices, such as smartphones and digital cameras, can be used to make preliminary anemia screening more accessible, convenient, and widely available. By leveraging technology that many people already have access to, we hope to explore a simple, non-invasive approach that could support earlier screening and encourage individuals to seek timely medical evaluation when needed.

🔬

Research-Driven Insights

Our research explores the relationship between external eye images and laboratory-measured hemoglobin levels to determine whether subtle visual indicators of anemia can be identified through advanced image analysis. By comparing eye images with clinical hemoglobin measurements, we aim to investigate the potential of a non-invasive, technology-driven approach for supporting early anemia screening and advancing research in accessible healthcare.
