HemaSight research prototype

Eye scan and symptom check

Answer a few anemia-related questions, then upload a clear eye scan. The app compares the image with labeled research images and returns a limited screening estimate.

Before you continue

This is not a medical test.

The image model has only been evaluated on a small adult research cohort. Its estimate can be wrong and cannot confirm or rule out anemia. A blood test is needed to measure hemoglobin.

The available image model is intended for adults aged 19–65. Other ages can complete the questions, but the app will not return an image estimate for them.

Privacy and data retention

This screening processes your answers and eye image in memory for the screening request. It does not save them as a patient record.

After you click Continue, you have 15 minutes to finish the screening. When time expires, the page clears your answers and selected image, and the server will reject any later submission. This app does not save them as a patient record. Do not include your name, face, or other identifying details in your upload.
